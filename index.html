About

Profile

I am a doctoral researcher in the Department of Astronomy, Astrophysics and Space Engineering (DAASE) at the Indian Institute of Technology Indore, and a member of the COSMOS group led by Dr. Priyanka Singh.

My research explores the connection between active galactic nuclei (AGN) and the circumgalactic medium (CGM). I investigate how AGN populate their dark matter halos, and how the feedback they release reshapes the gas reservoirs around galaxies.

More broadly, I am interested in how baryons are distributed around galaxies — and in the flows of matter and energy that drive galaxy evolution across cosmic time.

Current focus

I cross-correlate X-ray–selected AGN from eROSITA with galaxy samples from DESI to constrain the halo occupation distribution of AGN — revealing what kinds of dark matter halos host actively accreting black holes. In parallel, I study the circumgalactic medium and the cosmic distribution of baryons.

During a research visit to SISSA, Trieste, I am working with Prof. Andrea Lapi on spectral energy distribution (SED) fitting of galaxies using GalaPy.
